## Question Categories

### Behavioral Questions (10 questions)
Best answered using the STAR method. Focus on personal experiences.
- Examples: "Tell me about a time you overcame a challenge", "Describe a conflict with a teammate"

### Technical Questions (10 questions)
For technical roles. Discuss projects, tools, and problem-solving approaches.
- Examples: "Walk through your most complex project", "How do you debug issues?"

### Situational Questions (10 questions)
Hypothetical scenarios. Show your thinking and approach.
- Examples: "How would you handle a tight deadline?", "What if you disagreed with your manager?"

### Company Culture Questions (10 questions)
About the company, role fit, and expectations.
- Examples: "Why are you interested in our company?", "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

## STAR Method Deep Dive

### What is STAR?
**S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult - A framework for answering behavioral questions.

### Components:
1. **Situation**: Set the context
   - What was the scenario?
   - When did it happen?
   - What was the environment?

2. **Task**: Define the challenge
   - What was your responsibility?
   - What needed to be accomplished?
   - What were the constraints?

3. **Action**: Explain your approach
   - What specific actions did you take?
   - What was your thinking?
   - Focus on YOUR actions, not the team's

4. **Result**: Share the outcome
   - What was the result?
   - What metrics can you include?
   - What did you learn?

### STAR Story Template:
```
Situation: [2-3 sentences setting context]
Task: [1-2 sentences on your responsibility]
Action: [2-3 sentences on what YOU did]
Result: [1-2 sentences with metrics and learnings]
```

### Tips for STAR Stories:
- Prepare 5-7 good stories from your work experience
- Keep each story to 2-3 minutes
- Include quantifiable results when possible
- Practice out loud
- Focus on YOUR actions, not "we" or "the team"
- Show your thought process and problem-solving

## Interview Red Flags & Green Flags

### Green Flags (Good Signs):
✓ Interviewers ask about your growth and learning opportunities
✓ They're eager to answer your questions about the company
✓ Clear communication about role expectations
✓ Structured interview process with clear next steps
✓ Team members seem engaged and positive
✓ They ask about your career goals

### Red Flags (Warning Signs):
✗ Disorganized interview with unclear questions
✗ Interviewers seem unengaged or dismissive
✗ Vague or changing job descriptions
✗ No clear timeline communicated
✗ Inappropriate or discriminatory questions
✗ High employee turnover mentioned casually
✗ Salary only negotiable downward

### After Interview:
- Send thank you emails within 24 hours
- Reference specific conversations
- Reiterate your interest in the position
- Ask about next steps and timeline
- Follow up if you don't hear back as promised

## Interview Prep Checklist

### Research Phase (1-2 weeks before)
- [ ] Research the company thoroughly
- [ ] Understand the company's products/services
- [ ] Learn about the company culture
- [ ] Review recent company news
- [ ] Identify key competitors
- [ ] Understand the market and industry

### Self-Preparation Phase (1 week before)
- [ ] Prepare 5-7 STAR method stories
- [ ] Practice answers to common questions
- [ ] Prepare thoughtful questions for interviewers
- [ ] Review your resume completely
- [ ] Practice with mock interviews
- [ ] Research the interviewer (if known)

### Technical Preparation (if applicable)
- [ ] Review relevant technical concepts
- [ ] Practice coding/design problems
- [ ] Be ready to explain your technical background
- [ ] Prepare diagrams or examples
- [ ] Practice whiteboarding (if applicable)

### Day Before
- [ ] Confirm all interview details (time, location, format)
- [ ] Plan your commute/tech setup
- [ ] Prepare outfit and materials
- [ ] Get good sleep
- [ ] Review company info one more time

### Day Of
- [ ] Arrive 10-15 minutes early
- [ ] Silence your phone
- [ ] Bring multiple copies of your resume
- [ ] Bring a notepad and pen
- [ ] Take deep breaths and relax
- [ ] Bring energy and positive attitude

### Software Engineer

### Product Manager
- Discuss product strategy experience

### Manager/Leadership
- Prepare examples of team leadership
- Discuss how you've developed people
- Show conflict resolution examples
- Discuss building culture

### Data/Analytics
- Discuss data projects in detail
- Be ready to explain your analytical approach

- Discuss insights you've generated
- Be ready for statistical questions

## Common Mistake to Avoid

❌ Giving answers that are too long (keep to 2-3 minutes per story)
❌ Using "we" instead of "I" when telling your stories
❌ Not practicing out loud before the interview
❌ Focusing on company needs rather than your fit
❌ Not preparing questions for the interviewer
❌ Interviewing only one person from the company
❌ Asking only about salary/benefits immediately
❌ Not following up after the interview

## Best Practices

✓ Listen carefully before answering
✓ Use specific examples with metrics
✓ Show enthusiasm and energy
✓ Ask thoughtful questions
✓ Be honest if you don't know something
✓ Show your thought process
✓ Take notes during the interview
✓ Send thank you notes within 24 hours
✓ Negotiate thoughtfully (if offer comes)
✓ Keep professional relationships
